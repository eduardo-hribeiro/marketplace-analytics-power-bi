## 📌 About the Project

**Marketplace Analytics** is a Business Intelligence project developed in **Power BI** to analyze the performance of a Brazilian marketplace from multiple business perspectives.

The solution brings together information related to:

- sales and revenue;
- orders;
- products and categories;
- pricing;
- payment methods;
- customers;
- reviews;
- deliveries;
- logistics costs;
- performance over time.

The project was developed using **Power Query** for data preparation and transformation, **relational data modeling** to structure the different data sources, and **DAX** to create the metrics and KPIs used throughout the analysis.

In addition to the main dashboards, the report includes page navigation, filters, custom tooltips, drill-through functionality, and comparisons between individual categories and the overall marketplace performance.

> **Note:** The dashboard interface is presented in Portuguese, as the project was originally developed using a Brazilian e-commerce dataset. This documentation provides the full project overview in English.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of this project was to transform transactional marketplace data into an analytical solution capable of answering business questions such as:

- What is the total revenue generated by the marketplace?
- How many orders were placed?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- Which categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories have the highest sales volume?
- How have revenue and sales volume evolved over time?
- Which payment methods are most frequently used?
- What is the overall level of customer satisfaction?
- How do delivery delays affect customer reviews?
- What is the average delivery time?
- How does a specific product category perform compared with the marketplace average?

# 🧩 Data Modeling

The project uses a relational data model composed of the original dataset tables and auxiliary dimensions created during development.

![Data Model](images/09-modelo-dados.png)

The main tables include:

### Orders
`olist_orders_dataset`

Contains information related to orders, customers, dates, and delivery status.

### Order Items
`olist_order_items_dataset`

Contains products associated with each order, prices, sellers, and freight costs.

### Customers
`olist_customers_dataset`

Contains customer and location information.

### Products
`olist_products_dataset`

Contains information about products and their categories.

### Payments
`olist_order_payments_dataset`

Contains payment methods, transaction amounts, and installment information.

### Reviews
`olist_order_reviews_dataset`

Contains customer review data.

# 💡 Key Insights

The analysis revealed several relevant patterns in marketplace performance.

### 💰 Revenue & Sales

The marketplace generated approximately **R$13.6 million in revenue** during the analyzed period, distributed across approximately **99 thousand orders**.

The Average Order Value was approximately **R$137**.

### 📦 Product Profile

Approximately **113 thousand items** were sold, representing around **33 thousand distinct products** across **74 categories**.

A significant portion of the sales volume is concentrated among products priced below **R$100**.

### 🏆 Categories

Categories such as **Health & Beauty**, **Watches & Gifts**, and **Bed, Bath & Table** make a significant contribution to marketplace performance.

However, a category with a high sales volume does not necessarily generate the highest revenue, highlighting the importance of analyzing **price, volume, and revenue together**.

### 💳 Payments

**Credit cards** are by far the most frequently used payment method among marketplace customers.

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

The overall average review score is above **4 points**, with approximately **77% of reviews classified as positive**.

Maximum-score reviews account for a significant share of the total customer feedback.

### 🚚 Logistics & Customer Experience

The analysis reveals a significant difference in customer satisfaction between orders delivered on time and delayed orders.

Orders delivered within the expected timeframe have higher average review scores, while delayed orders receive considerably lower ratings.

This indicates a strong relationship between **logistics efficiency and customer experience**.
